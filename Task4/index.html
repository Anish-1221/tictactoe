About this task

Alpha-beta needs two sides, and with three players there aren't two. So each player searches as if the other two are working together against it (the "paranoid" approach). That gives a single score again, my score, which I maximise and both opponents minimise, and normal alpha-beta works.

Moves are tried in this order: a move that wins, then a move that blocks someone else's win, then the rest by number of open paths through the cell.

With all three players at the same depth, every game is a draw. With three in a row on a 4×4 board, any line one player starts can be blocked by either of the other two. Lower one player's depth to see wins.

The maxn option is there for comparison. It gives every player its own score and lets each one maximise its own, which is more realistic but can't use alpha-beta. On the same positions it searched roughly 2 to 15 times as many nodes.
